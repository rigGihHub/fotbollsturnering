# v431 – Manual choice safeguards

- CupNavi group proposals are explicitly previews and never write before an explicit button action.
- The fallback recommended-group action is secondary, not primary.
- Schedule generation now states exactly what it may change and what it preserves.
- Re-generating an existing unplayed schedule requires explicit confirmation because times, pitches and referee assignments may change.
- Team group placement, team data and saved competition rules are explicitly preserved by the schedule action.
