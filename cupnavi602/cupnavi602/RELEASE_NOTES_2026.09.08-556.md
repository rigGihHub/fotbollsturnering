# CupNavi v556 – Conditional halftime/period break

- Renames the break field to **Paus mellan halvlekar/perioder**.
- The halftime/period-break control is only rendered when the match has 2 or more halves/periods.
- With one half/period, the irrelevant break control is hidden while the saved value is preserved for future format changes.
- Retains v555: new-cup handoff to Lag, synchronized pitch times default, and optional pitch addresses unless travel planning is enabled.
