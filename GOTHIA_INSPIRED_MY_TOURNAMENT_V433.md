# v433 – Gothia-inspired My Tournament

Research benchmark: Gothia Cup official app/web.

## Adopted now
- "Mitt lag" becomes a personal tournament schedule, not just a team selector.
- Upcoming matches are shown chronologically and grouped by day.
- The next match is explicitly marked.
- Pitch remains visible on every row and existing directions are preserved.

## Strong ideas already present in CupNavi
- Follow/favorite team and notifications.
- Live results and match events.
- Team-focused match filtering.
- Pitch navigation.
- Responsive playoff presentation.
- Team-leader self-service.

## Candidates, not copied blindly
1. Universal public search across team/match/pitch.
2. Favorite pitches in addition to favorite team.
3. Age-class/statistics navigation for very large tournaments.
4. Offline status/read-only cache for matchday resilience.
5. Streaming links only if organizers have a real provider.

News/video/editorial feeds are deliberately not prioritized: they do not strengthen CupNavi's core job for ordinary organizers.
