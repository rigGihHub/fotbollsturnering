# v575 – Form UX Priority Hierarchy

Goal: reduce decision load for inexperienced cup organisers without removing advanced control.

## Principle
Every core setup form should make three levels obvious:
1. **Must be right now** – the minimum needed to move forward safely.
2. **Can be filled later** – useful metadata that must not block progress.
3. **Advanced / fine tuning** – specialist settings hidden until needed.

## Changes
- Cupinfo now explains what the guided setup needs now, what can wait and what is advanced.
- Lag makes lagnamn (and class when relevant) the only immediate task. Kit/contact/travel data is explicitly deferred.
- Tröj setup remains a secondary, later action rather than competing with adding teams.
- Regler keeps match length and points visible; tie-breaks, playoff details and scheduling principles are progressively disclosed.
- Existing schedule-safety and played-match protection are unchanged.
