# CupNavi 2026.09.10-608 — Comic × Football Cards × Text-TV × Future

## New signature direction
- Replaces the dark Broadcast Control shell with a light-first CupNavi identity.
- Mixes four visual references deliberately: comic panels, football trading cards, Swedish Text-TV and restrained future UI.
- Uses warm paper rather than sterile white, dark ink for long-form readability and a small, semantic accent palette.
- Adds subtle halftone / collectible-card detailing only in hero surfaces.
- Uses firm dark outlines and offset shadows to create an ownable illustrated/card construction without cartoon clutter.
- Keeps Text-TV data surfaces intentionally black as the live/broadcast layer.
- Reworks buttons, panels, inputs, alerts and admin setup tabs for high contrast in all states.
- Accessibility rule: decorative treatments may never lower text contrast.
