# CupNavi v606 — Premium Sports Design System

## Direction
Nordic Sports Control Room × Text-TV 330.

## Palette
- Canvas `#07141D` / deep chrome `#041018`
- Surface `#0D1F2A`, raised `#122A37`
- Primary text `#F4F8FA`, secondary `#B7C8D0`
- Interaction cyan `#3BC7D6`
- Success `#43C77A`, warning `#F2C14E`, danger `#F06A6A`
- Text-TV data surface `#010506`

The palette is deliberately less neon than v605. Cyan is interaction, green is success/commit, and black is reserved for dense sport data.
